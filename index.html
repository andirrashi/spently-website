Spently Support

Say it. Snap it. Done. Spently turns a spoken sentence, a typed amount or a photographed receipt into an expense, and answers questions about where your money goes. Everything stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

Need help? Email andirrashi@gmail.com. Spently is made by one person, so replies usually take a day or two. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Found a bug or have an idea? The same address works for that too.

Requirements

  • An iPhone running iOS 26 or later.
  • Voice entry needs microphone and speech recognition access, which Spently asks for the first time you use the mic.
  • Apple Intelligence is optional. On an iPhone that supports it, with Apple Intelligence turned on in the Settings app, Spently reads sentences, receipts and questions with Apple's on-device model. On other iPhones it uses its own built-in rules, which understand English phrasing such as "coffee 3.50" or "spent 20 on lunch yesterday".
  • iCloud sync needs an iPhone signed in to iCloud. It is on by default and can be turned off in Settings › Data.

Getting started

The home screen has three buttons at the bottom:

  • Camera scans a receipt. Press and hold it to choose a photo instead.
  • Keyboard opens the entry form: amount, currency, category, date, and optional merchant and note.
  • Mic: tap it and say what you spent, for example "Spent 12 on a taxi" or "coffee 3.50 and lunch 14". It finishes when you stop talking, or tap it again. You can also hold it while you speak and let go when you're done. The entries are then saved. If Spently can't tell the category, it opens the entry for you to choose one.

Tap the period title at the top to move between months, weeks or years. Tap the chart button for the Breakdown, and the gear for Settings.

Questions and answers

What can I say to the mic?

Any sentence with an amount in it. Spently looks for the amount, a currency if you name one, a category or subcategory name, a merchant after "at", "from" or "to", and a day such as "yesterday". Several entries in one sentence work: "coffee 3.50 and a taxi 12". You can also say a count and a price: "2 beers at 4 each".

Say "income" or "salary" for money coming in, for example "salary 2,400 from Acme".

Questions work from the same mic: "How much did I spend on food this month?", "What was my biggest expense last week?", "How much do I spend on coffee a month?"

How do I use Siri?

Spently works with Siri without any setup. Try:

  • "Add to Spently" or "Add an expense in Spently", then say the entry.
  • "Log a purchase in Spently"
  • "Ask Spently how much I spent on food this month"
  • "Check my spending in Spently"

Complete entries are saved straight away and Siri reads them back. Anything Siri couldn't place in a category waits in Spently for you to finish. The same actions are available in the Shortcuts app and can be assigned to the Action button.

How does receipt scanning work?

Tap the camera button and photograph the receipt, or press and hold the button to pick an existing photo. Spently reads the merchant, total, date and the items on the receipt, picks a category, and opens the entry for you to check before saving. Nothing is saved without you seeing it.

If items on one receipt belong to different categories (groceries and drinks, say), Spently offers to split it into one entry per category.

The receipt image itself is not kept. Reading happens on your iPhone.

How do categories and subcategories work?

Spently starts with a set of expense and income categories such as Food, Transport and Salary, some with subcategories (Food › Lunch, Transport › Taxi). Manage them in Settings › Categories: rename, change the emoji, reorder, add subcategories, or delete. Deleting a category asks where its entries and repeating entries should move; nothing is deleted with it.

Over time Spently learns from your own entries: a merchant you've used before fills in its category, and a word you've used before ("smokes") finds the category you gave it last time.

Repeating entries and installments

When adding an entry, tap "Repeat or Split Into Installments". A repeat (weekly, monthly or yearly) creates a new entry automatically each time it's due, on the same day of the month where possible. Installments split one purchase into monthly payments, labeled 1/12 to 12/12. A yearly subscription can be both: it renews each year and is paid in monthly installments.

Manage them in Settings › Repeating Entries and Settings › Installments, where you can pause, edit, end or cancel remaining payments. Future payments show on the home screen as upcoming; turn "Show Upcoming" off in Settings if you'd rather see only what's been paid.

Currencies and exchange rates

Spently picks your home currency from your iPhone's region the first time it runs. Every entry keeps the currency it was recorded in; totals are shown in whichever currency you pick with the pill under the total on the home screen.

Add or remove currencies, change the home currency and check exchange rates in Settings › Currencies. Rates are refreshed once a day from a public source, or you can type your own. A currency without a rate is left out of totals until it has one, and the home screen says so.

If you've allowed location, Spently notices when you're in a country with a different currency and offers to record new entries in it until you're home.

What is in the Breakdown?

Spending over time with a tappable bar chart, highlights (daily average, projection, comparison with the previous period, biggest day), where it went by category, what changed most, fixed versus flexible costs, weekday and time-of-day habits, the largest expenses, income next to expenses, and written insights. Pick any timeframe, including custom dates, and hide the sections you don't need with Customize.

Asking questions by typing

Open search on the home screen and type a question instead of a search term, for example "how much on groceries last month?". Tap Search on the keyboard and the answer appears above the entries it's based on.

iCloud sync

With iCloud Sync on (Settings › Data), entries, categories and repeating entries are kept in your private iCloud and appear on every device signed in with the same Apple Account. They also come back when you set up a new iPhone. Sync usually takes a few seconds; after a fresh install give it a minute.

Turning sync off keeps everything on the iPhone and stops syncing from the next launch. Data already in iCloud stays there until you remove it in the Settings app under your name › iCloud.

Backup and restore

Settings › Backup & Data › Back Up… writes one file with every entry, category and repeating entry, and your currencies. Restoring it on a new iPhone brings the currencies too. Save it to Files, iCloud Drive or anywhere you like. Restore From Backup… reads it back; restoring only adds what isn't already there and never removes or changes existing entries, so it's safe to run more than once.

Backups include saved locations, so share them only where you'd share your entries.

Importing a CSV file

Settings › Backup & Data › Import CSV File… reads a spreadsheet export with one entry per row and these columns:

date,kind,category,subcategory,note,amount,currency
2026-03-01T15:28:12,expense,Coffee,,,1.60,EUR
2026-03-01T15:30:01,expense,Food,Lunch,Team lunch,30,EUR
2026-03-01T09:00:00,income,Salary,,,2400,EUR

date is ISO 8601 in local time, kind is expense or income, and subcategory and note may be empty. Categories that don't exist yet are created. Rows already imported are skipped.

Widgets and Control Center

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget and choose Spently. Spending shows your total, money in and out, your trend or top categories for the period you pick. There are also Quick Add, Recent, Upcoming and Category widgets, several of them for the Lock Screen too.

In Control Center, add Add to Spently and choose whether it opens Spently to speak, type, scan a receipt or add income. It works on the Action button as well.

Face ID Lock

Turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security. Spently then asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode when it opens and when you come back to it, at once or after the delay you choose under Require Face ID. While it's on, widgets show only that Spently is locked, and the Category widget can't list your categories to choose from.

Exporting to a spreadsheet

Settings › Backup & Data › Export CSV File… saves every entry as a CSV file that Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets can open. Import CSV File… reads one back, skipping entries that are already there.

Saving where an entry was recorded

Save Location starts off. Allowing location access when you tap Finish on the last setup page enables it if you have not already chosen a setting. You can change it later in Settings › Entries. With it on, each entry you add remembers where you were and shows it on a small map when you open the entry. The location is only read at the moment you save; Spently never tracks you in the background. Turn it off any time.

"Possible repeat" when saving

Spently warns when a new entry looks like one you added moments ago or the same merchant and amount today, to catch a double tap or a receipt scanned twice. Tap Add Anyway if it's genuinely a second purchase. After two Add Anyways for the same category and amount in a day, it stops asking about that pair until tomorrow.

Deleting entries or everything

Swipe left on an entry to delete it, or open it and tap Delete Entry. An installment entry can delete just that payment or the whole plan. Settings › Backup & Data › Erase All Entries deletes every entry and repeating entry, on this iPhone and, with iCloud Sync on, on your other devices. Categories stay.

Troubleshooting

The mic says "Denied" or doesn't record

Open the Settings app › Apps › Spently and allow Microphone and Speech Recognition. Spently's own Settings › Voice & Apple Intelligence shows the current state and has a shortcut there.

The mic says it's still getting ready

On first use iOS downloads the speech model for your language, which needs the internet once. Wait a moment and tap the mic again. After that voice works offline.

Apple Intelligence shows "Turned Off" or "Not Supported"

Apple Intelligence needs a supported iPhone, a supported language, and the switch turned on in the Settings app › Apple Intelligence & Siri. Spently works without it; only the understanding of unusual phrasings and foreign-language sentences is better with it.

Entries don't appear on my other device

Check both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud Sync is on in Spently's Settings › Data on both, and that iCloud Drive is enabled in the Settings app under your name › iCloud. Open Spently on both devices and give it a minute. A device without a network connection syncs as soon as it's back online.

A receipt was read wrong

Every scanned receipt opens for checking before it's saved, so correct the amount, date or category there. Flat, well-lit receipts with the total visible read best. Receipts in languages other than English are read best with Apple Intelligence on.

A total leaves out some entries

Entries in a currency without an exchange rate can't be converted, and the home screen says which currency is missing. Open Settings › Currencies › Exchange Rates and tap Update Now, or type the rate yourself.

Privacy

Spently has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your entries stay on your devices and in your private iCloud, and the developer cannot see them. The full privacy policy explains what each feature uses and where it goes.
